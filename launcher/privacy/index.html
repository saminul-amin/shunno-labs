Privacy Policy — Shunno Launcher

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Shunno Launcher is published by Shunno Labs. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Shunno Launcher keeps your launcher data on your device, without analytics, advertising, or trackers.

  • No analytics, no advertising, no trackers, no accounts, no in-app purchases.
  • Everything you configure (home apps, categories, notes, todos, profiles, settings) stays on your device. We have no servers and receive none of your data.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device.

Data that leaves your device

  • Weather: nothing is requested until you choose a city, and you can turn weather off in settings. When you search for a city, the name you type is sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding API. The app then requests current conditions from the Open-Meteo weather API using the coordinates of the city you chose. Requests use HTTPS and contain no identifiers. Your device's own location is never used for weather. See Open-Meteo's terms.
  • Web search shortcuts: when you choose to search the web, Google Play, or YouTube from the search bar, your query is opened in that app or your browser. From then on, that service's own privacy policy applies.

Nothing else is sent anywhere.

Permissions and what they are used for

Every item below is used only on your device.

  • Installed apps: the app list is read to show your apps on the home screen and in the app drawer. It is the core function of a launcher.
  • Usage access: used to show your own screen time, to run the Pause feature and app time limits, and — only if you turn on “Include device activity” — to help order app search results.
  • Adaptive app ranking: off by default. Only while it is on does Shunno record which apps you open from Shunno, as a decaying count and a timestamp per app; with it off, nothing is written. The record orders equally relevant search results only, and never overrides a better text match. At most 200 apps are kept, entries expire after 90 days. Turn it off or erase what was learned under Settings > Search > Adaptive ranking.
  • Contacts: optional. Read only to show matching contacts when you search. Contacts are not copied or stored by the app.
  • Calendar: optional. Read only to show your next event on the home screen. Events are not copied or stored by the app.
  • Physical activity (step counter): optional. Steps are read from the device sensor to show your step count, and stored only on your device.
  • Accessibility service: optional, and enabled only after an in-app explanation you agree to. Used exclusively to lock the screen with your lock gesture, and to open the notification shade on phones where the usual method does not work. It cannot read screen content, and it collects and stores nothing.
  • Device administrator: optional, an alternative way to lock the screen. Used only for locking the screen.
  • Shizuku: optional. If you install and allow Shizuku, it is used only to lock the screen and to switch grayscale mode.
  • Display over other apps: optional. Used only to show the Pause screen over an app you chose to pause.
  • Notification access: optional, used for monochrome home-screen indicators. While enabled, app identity, profile, category and notification status are processed in memory on your device. Message text is not inspected; notification contents and history are not saved or shared. Turning indicators off clears their in-memory state and stops processing notifications. You can also revoke notification access in Android settings.
  • Biometric app lock: optional. Fingerprint or face checks are performed by Android. The app only learns whether the check passed and never receives biometric data.
  • Notifications and alarms: used to deliver reminders you create, and to show the notice that Pause is running.
  • Location and Wi-Fi state (launcher profiles): profile configuration and configured Wi-Fi names stay on your device and are included in backups you export. Android may require precise location permission and device Location to expose the connected Wi-Fi name. Permission is requested only from the explanatory Wi-Fi access action in profile settings. Shunno does not collect coordinates, scan Wi-Fi, request background location, or send Wi-Fi names anywhere. Current connection information is transient; manual overrides save matching rule IDs, not connection history. Schedule rules use one inexact alarm at the next transition.

Backups and crash reports

  • Backups: created only when you ask, saved to a file location you choose.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, the error details are saved on your device so you can view them in Settings. They are shared only if you choose to share them yourself.
